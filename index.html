CohortLens Technical Guide & Methodology

Product Overview

CohortLens is an enterprise-grade client-side analytics processor built specifically for SaaS founders, Fractional CFOs, and M&A diligence auditors. It transforms unnormalized transaction ledgers into standard N-Month cohort retention heatmaps and monthly MRR waterfalls without connecting bank credentials or third-party webhooks.

Accepted Ledger Format

You can drag and drop exports directly from Stripe (Charges/Invoices), Chargebee, Paddle, or custom warehouse extracts. CohortLens looks for:

  • Customer Identifier: e.g., customer_id, email, user_id
  • Timestamp / Date: e.g., created_at, date (ISO 8601 or YYYY-MM-DD preferred)
  • Amount / MRR: e.g., amount, mrr, value (normalized to dollars)

Metric Methodology

  • Cohort Month: First timestamp where positive revenue is recorded for a distinct customer.
  • Month N Retention %: Distinct active customers in month N divided by initial cohort size.
  • MRR Waterfall: Segregates New (first ever), Expansion (positive delta), Contraction (negative delta), and Churn ($0 after active).
  • SaaS Quick Ratio: (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Contraction MRR + Churn MRR).

Zero Cloud Data Leakage

This application operates purely inside client memory. Once the page is loaded, you can disconnect your internet and execute complex multi-thousand row audits offline.
